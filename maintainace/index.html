The Product

Turning Fisheries Data Into Business Intelligence.

The Fish Demand Forecasting & Inventory Management System is a custom-built digital platform developed to power the operations of Jagdamba Fisheries. It combines modern web technology, Data Science, Machine Learning, analytics and automation to transform operational data into actionable business intelligence.

The platform is designed to help the business understand demand, maintain optimal inventory, analyze sales, improve logistics and make faster, more informed decisions across its operations.

The Challenge

Why This
System Exists.

The core challenge addressed by this platform is the inefficiency and lack of data-driven strategy in managing fisheries export operations.


Traditional manual systems make it difficult for management to obtain a clear real-time view of operational performance. Important information such as daily revenue, top client sales, product profitability and business-center performance can become difficult to access and analyze.


Repetitive tasks such as preparing daily reports and performing complex GST calculations consume valuable employee time. Logistics planning can also become inefficient, particularly when determining optimal vehicle utilization.


Manual data entry introduces another major challenge: inconsistent and inaccurate records. Without automated monitoring and anomaly detection, unusual transactions or operational mistakes can remain unnoticed.


Inventory management is another critical area. Inaccurate stock tracking can lead to shortages, delayed orders and strained client relationships.

Project Objective

Building a Smarter Fisheries Operation.

The primary objective of the Fish Demand Forecasting & Inventory Management System is to implement a complete web-based management platform that brings analytics, prediction and automation to Jagdamba Fisheries. The system is designed to improve operational efficiency, strengthen inventory planning, support accurate business decisions and ultimately contribute to increased profitability.

By combining historical data with predictive intelligence, the platform creates a foundation where business decisions can be based on measurable information rather than assumptions or guesswork.
